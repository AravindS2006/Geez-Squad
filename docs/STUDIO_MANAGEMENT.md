# Coaching workspace and studio management

Updated 11 October 2026.

The coach overview now supports daily coaching decisions: current studio members, live and upcoming sessions in the next seven days, trainees with a check-in in the last 24 hours, and follow-ups. Follow-ups highlight recent reports of low energy or high soreness, missing plan assignments, and established members without saved activity for seven days. Missing check-ins are not interpreted as health problems. Saved activity is not attendance. Coaches can review the full follow-up roster or open an individual profile.

The trainee overview shows personal training days, completed sets, observed active minutes, upcoming sessions, the assigned plan, goal, and check-in tools. Progress has four primary measures; detailed reps, holds, tracking coverage, and reported load are available on request. Coaches see per-trainee progress instead of an average camera score across different people and exercises. Exercise quality, range, and tempo remain grouped by movement and rule version. CSV export remains available. Detailed views retain the documented limit of the latest 500 saved sets; each studio member's latest check-in, last activity, and seven-day completed-set count are fetched separately so a busy trainee cannot hide another member's follow-up.

In Sessions, coaches can edit a scheduled meeting's title, local date/time, duration, plan, capacity, and trainee roster. Existing enrollments are preserved when they remain selected. Removed enrollees lose access; newly selected studio members see the session on their calendars. Server-side validation enforces ownership, current membership, valid plans, and the limit of 100 trainees. Live, completed, and cancelled sessions cannot be rescheduled. A version derived from editable fields, roster, status, and revision prevents two editors from silently overwriting each other. Schedule/roster changes also invalidate a concurrent stale class-start operation. LiveKit room preparation uses the platform enrollment ceiling so an empty room prepared by an older start request cannot retain an obsolete lower capacity; application enrollment and consent still gate token issuance.

Trainee profiles expose shared name and goal editing, assigned plans, private coach notes, recent check-ins, saved activity, and a direct link to personal progress. Name/goal edits detect concurrent profile changes. Account email and authentication credentials are outside this editor. New members join through the existing email-bound invitation link; the platform does not send email when delivery is deferred.

Removing a studio member requires a deliberate confirmation and is blocked while they have a live enrollment. The transaction removes studio membership, plan assignments, pending unused invitation links, and scheduled enrollments. It preserves the person's account, personal workout/check-in records, and completed-class enrollment history. The former coach loses access to their personal progress and check-ins. Upcoming class revisions change to invalidate open roster editors. Audit events record management changes. No database migration is required.

Verification includes 266 unit cases, 168 real local authentication/API checks, and a mobile browser journey that edits a meeting, swaps its roster, verifies both trainees' calendars, edits a shared profile, and removes future access. The existing assigned-plan/check-in/history/retry journey and classroom gallery tests remain part of acceptance. The optimized build, all 38 Edge browser tests, 20 accessibility scans, formatting, dependency audit, and both deferred-email authentication modes passed locally. CI verifies the final commit before live publication; canonical Vercel/Neon acceptance uses two scoped temporary accounts that are removed after the checks.

These workflows do not establish physical pose accuracy or lag-free operation with 30 independent devices. The transport and camera validation boundaries in [CLASSROOM_UPGRADE.md](CLASSROOM_UPGRADE.md) and [VERIFICATION.md](VERIFICATION.md) continue to apply.
